# Panel A2 order

Select a single **150 × 150 mm**, two-layer FR-4 board, 1.6 mm thick, 1 oz copper per side, green mask, white legend and ENIG. Standard two-layer fabrication and top-side PCBA are sufficient. Use the four 3.2 mm NPTH mounting holes at 12 mm from the nearest edges and the five separate plated SMA holes. Keep the loop fully covered by solder mask. Preserve the empty aperture and the small return connection behind the SMA. Request flying-probe testing with the intentional etched L401 connection preserved.

Use the 0.10 mm top stencil with the supplied paste. Leave C405 empty. Its available lands allow coarse series adjustment. Fit C401 820 pF and C402 68 pF on the shunt side, C404 270 pF in the series bank, C403/C406 JZ200HV trimmers, R401 2.2 Ω and J401. The two marks identify the rotor land. With RF off, use an insulated adjustment tool. Leave overhead clearance for both trimmer screws and the vertical SMA. Route the coax with strain relief outside the loop aperture.

Check the [full BOM](assembly/Panel_A2-full-BOM.csv), [local assembly list](assembly/Panel_A2-manual-and-DNP.csv) and [assembly drawing](assembly/Panel_A2-assembly.pdf). The matched JZ200HV may require JLC preorder. Source the exact trimmers for local reflow if unavailable, and remove those same two designators from both factory CSVs for that alternate order. Do not fit C405 when populating the fixed capacitors. L401 is manufactured as nine positive front-copper strokes with a 12 mm center-to-center feed gap. A capacitor network and resistor close the circuit at the feed. CAM inspection checks each stroke against its endpoints.
