# Rev A2 physical acceptance record

Use one worksheet per board, label, panel, patch and cavity location. Record date, operator, serials, component changes, input voltages, current limits, ambient temperature, probe loading, cable length and calibration planes. Preserve raw scope traces, VNA sweeps, protocol journals, raster hash and camera image together. The following are acceptance tests for fabricated hardware.

1. **Assembly and power.** Inspect side, polarity, relay type, pin 1, RF transformer dots and trimmer rotor. Check continuity at all five RF contacts, and check against the schematic for shorts. Verify reservoir value, R106, and C205 ≥1 µF at 3.3 V bias. Begin at 23 ±2 °C with J2 open, both RF patches open and J209 off. Apply J1 at 5.25 V ±1%, initially limited to 50 mA to screen shorts. Increase only as needed, up to 0.50 A. Check PRE_3V3, switched-rail discharge and host pin states. Measure U1 dissipation and package temperature at full duty. Keep measured package temperature below 70 °C and the conservative junction estimate below 100 °C. Record the host pins and boot states for the selected Pi or ESP32.
2. **Cold selection and interlocks.** With SET/RESET/RUN low and TP301 high, clear each addressed relay with a 20 ms RESET, then SET one. Use J5 for second-pole readback and an ohmmeter on the actual RF pole. Repeat for ports 0–4. Open, zero-port, two-port, lost host power, floating controls and invalid addresses must keep RF off. Verify ARM stays below 0.5 V when inhibited and exceeds 1.2 V only after the required settle interval. Exercise stuck RUN, address changes with hostile SET/RESET and chatter. Require all coil outputs quiet whenever TP301 is low. Switch only after field decay.
3. **Ordering under faults.** With a 50 Ω load, measure COIL_RES, K200 coil voltage, ARM, RF_3V3, TP301 and J202 envelope on simultaneous traces. Mark contact make/break electrically. Test fast loss and slow ramps at J1, J209 and HOST_3V3, individually and in combinations, plus removal of J2 and invalid feedback while running. Check RF_3V3 falls from 3.45 V to ≤0.30 V in ≤1.64 ms or recalculate from measured capacitance and a tighter initial level. Require ≤20 µF equivalent switched capacitance, ≤30 mA reservoir drain, ≥4 V directly across K200 until RF is quiet, and matched-load carrier below 30 mV peak before **any** contact movement. Record margin and contact bounce. Verify no renewed coil pulse until 12 ms of quiet qualification. Observe every dead rail including reader pins and require ≤0.30 V settled. Repeat with PA energized and the host absent. A weak rail that cannot qualify must leave ARM low. Stop on any failed condition.
4. **Direct RF first.** Power down and patch J201–J202. Keep J209 disconnected. Connect a calibrated directional coupler and 50 Ω dummy load to the one selected port. Verify 27.12 MHz CLK, SPI readback and six version bytes with the [Pi probe](../../firmware/README.md). Start the reader at minimum drive. Record the TX differential waveform, loaded TVDD current, forward/reflected power, harmonics and modulation sidebands. Reject clipping or source overshoot beyond the design envelope. Scope RXP/RXN relative to VMID with short, low-capacitance probes; use the NXP recommended 1.5–1.65 V input level and absolute limits for this silicon. Capture a clean first response after TX and successful repeated activation. Adjust R203/R204 symmetrically when required.
5. **PA into a dummy load.** Power down, remove the direct patch, connect J201–J210 and J211–J202. Apply a regulated 24 V ±5%, current limit 0.15 A at J209, with J2 open. Measure both supply edges at U201 (<1 V/µs), quiescent current, REF and PD. Enabling must exceed REF + 1.5 V, disabling must fall below REF + 0.8 V. Check absence of autonomous oscillation with a wideband scope and spectrum analyzer, including no input. Raise the on-board source gradually to ≤25 mW forward, J210 ≤1.50 V RMS and ≤4.25 Vpp. Stop at 85 mW forward, excessive return power, thermal limit or loss of demodulation. Verify stability and receiver recovery under representative mismatch and cold-patched open/short fault fixtures using attenuated drive first. Record PA, R214, load and regulator temperatures after a sustained refresh-length dwell. Scope PA turnoff and K200 under each supply-loss case again.
6. **Panel and installation.** Measure bare loop L/R and the assembled panel sweep at instrument power. Record unloaded and mounted resonances, Q, return loss, bandwidth across ISO 14443-A sidebands, and trimmer setting. Calibrate at the controller connector, then repeat through the installed coax, ferrite, cabinet metal and representative product loading. Use an insulated tuning tool. De-energize before changing C402/C405 or the cable. Measure voltage and temperature on both trimmers, fixed caps, R401 and all inactive panels. Start with direct drive; stop on rising temperature, poor match or degraded receive margin. Record inactive-loop coupling and contact pulse count for life tracking.
7. **Label evidence.** Photograph the actual Santek model and orientation, then record its factory configuration and device ID with a known-good PC/SC unit. Run one known raster at close range and collect identity, all block acknowledgements, observed busy, stable idle and an independent camera image. On field loss report unknown and inspect the screen before replay. Use the same antenna throughout the full transfer/refresh window. For the custom SPI path first validate activation, ATS-derived timing, WTX, incoming/outgoing chaining, timeout handling and modulation recovery against the reference. A Pi version probe and offline test suite exercise different operations. [Vendor evidence](../research/VENDOR-PAYLOAD.md) · [Bench tool](../../software/README.md)
8. **Range.** Increase separation only after a repeatable direct, close-range camera-confirmed refresh. Record geometry, field continuity, failures and retries at each distance and orientation with all five ports available. The 305 mm target requires a full verified display refresh in the installed metal enclosure. See the [coverage matrix](PLAN.md).
