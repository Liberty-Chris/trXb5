# Rev A3 engineering review

The 160 × 112 mm controller and 150 × 150 mm antenna panel use top-side JLCPCB Standard mixed assembly. The two jobs use public inventory for every fitted component. The review covers two assembled controllers and ten interchangeable panels, one complete five-panel set per controller. Inventory is a dated observation. A successful build, contact measurements and a camera-confirmed refresh require hardware. ## Panel

C401/C402 provide 820 + 68 pF shunt. C404/C405 provide 220 + 39 pF series. All four are 500 V, 5% Yageo C0G parts in 1206. Each bank has a factory-fitted Knowles Voltronics JZ400HV ceramic trimmer. The rotor is pad 2, identified by the marked end, on RETURN for C403 and FEED for C406. R401 remains 2.2 Ω ±1%, 250 mW. The six capacitors, resistor and SMA are fitted. The aperture and etched winding use the A2 geometry. [Assembly drawing](../../releases/RevA3/panel/assembly/Panel_A3-assembly.pdf)

At nominal fixed values the shunt range is 896–928 pF and the series range is 267–299 pF. For the 0.64 µH, 1 Ω loop example with 2.2 Ω damping the calculated match is 897.71/277.54 pF, requiring about 9.71/18.54 pF of trim. Fixed-cap tolerance and installed metal, ferrite and coax can move the match outside these ranges. C402 and C405 remain accessible for coarse changes. The 350 V working rating and tool access are retained. The manufacturer’s V-70513 rev C drawing specifies a **200 minimum Q at 1 MHz** and −1500 ±1000 ppm/°C for the JZ400HV. The A2 JZ200HV was specified at 500 and 0 ±500 ppm/°C. Use the manufacturer's limits ahead of JLC's catalog description. The approximately 600 MHz self-resonance is above the carrier. At the example 85 mW operating point and 6.88 V RMS series voltage, the series trimmer carries about 10.9 mA RMS. Validate capacitor temperature, bandwidth, return loss and modulation sidebands with the actual assembly. [Manufacturer drawing, electrical table and recommended land](https://www.knowlescapacitors.com/getattachment/Products/Capacitors/Trimmer/Half-Turn/JZ-Series/Knowles-JZ-Series-V-70513-JZ_High-Voltage-%281%29.pdf?lang=en-US) · [Installed-panel calculation](../../software/panel_match.py)

At 13.56 MHz the loss contributed by the series trimmer to its parallel capacitor bank can be estimated as `Ctrim / (2π f Cbank² Qtrim)` when Q is large. For 18.54 pF in 277.54 pF this gives 0.014 Ω at an assumed Q of 200, or 0.141 Ω at Q of 20, compared with 3.2 Ω modeled loop-plus-damping resistance. These are sensitivity estimates. Measure installed Q and temperature because the specified 1 MHz Q does not establish the operating-frequency loss.

## Manufacturing verification

The public JLCPCB catalog and each exact part-detail page supply procurement quantity and process. The snapshot records the lower of stock and available-to-presell, clipped at zero. Per job, reserve the largest of five spares, the published loss count or 3% of fitted demand rounded up, then increase to the listed minimum patch quantity if necessary. Shared parts consume both jobs' reserves. All 73 MPNs meet that test. Public-stock matching is distinct from inventory reservation or a completed order. Preorder and consignment quantities in this build are zero. [Dated snapshot](../../hardware/RevA3/catalog.json) · [JLCPCB assembly FAQ](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-faqs-part-2)

Every fitted reference is checked against the numbered pads of its exact part-library revision. The audit compares the native and catalog copper centers, finds rotation and centroid offset, and checks the top side and insertion technology. It maps the SMA factory's four outer terminals to shell and its center to signal. It treats the 0.20 mm plated heat paths embedded in exposed pads as vias. The largest centroid difference, 0.438 mm on the TI DDA leads, is along the gull-wing land length. U301 uses the TI DYY drawing and C403/C406 use V-70513's 0.9 × 1.4 mm lands on 3.9 mm centers. The export corrects pin-1-based through-hole origins to their actual body centers. [Per-placement result](RevA3/placement.csv) · [Numbered library evidence](../../hardware/RevA3/lands.json)

Both designs have empty DRC exclusion lists. The final KiCad ERC and DRC JSON reports also have empty ignored-check lists. Enabled the inherited optional checks for courtyards, footprint metadata, tuning geometry and via centering; centered the short SCK and switched-rail track ends at the reader vias. The native pad/technology and placement checks independently cover the assembly mapping. The panel’s etched loop and bare test pads are PCB features. CAM checks the unobstructed central 110 × 90 mm area, all nine winding segments and the feed-side population outside that area. The outlines, SMA drills and four unplated mounts per board were independently read from the upload ZIPs. Gerbonara recognizes KiCad’s absolute-coordinate G90 following the Excellon tool header and reports a parser warning. The decoded drill coordinates and diameters are recorded in the CAM report.
