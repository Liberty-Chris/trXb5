# Rev A2 electrical and manufacturing review

Rev A2 targets an instrumented close-range prototype. The controller is 160 × 112 mm and the interchangeable primary and external panels are each 150 × 150 mm. J301 is the primary, J302–J305 the four optional paths. The five latching relays terminate inactive cables locally and a separate spring-return relay disconnects both reader drive and pickup. Two RF patch arrangements use one bus input. A Raspberry Pi or ESP32 supplies 3.3 V host logic through the same connectors. [Native controller](../../hardware/controller/RevA2/Controller_A2.kicad_pro) · [Native panel](../../hardware/antenna/RevA2/Panel_A2.kicad_pro) · [Issue and A1 review](https://github.com/Liberty-Chris/trXb5/issues/8)

TPS22918 recognizes low through 0.5 V, leaving 0.325 V margin. A supervisor sinking ARM guarantees at most 0.4 V at 1 mA above its specified minimum supply. |

## RF and coil permissives

Use J1 at 5.25 V ±1%, current limit 0.50 A, with a separate 3.3 V host and common ground. F1 is a 0.5 A hold PTC. D1 protects against reversed J1. U1 supplies PRE_3V3. Closing J2 permits the host to request RF. Its open state and the default-low isolator outputs leave RUN_REQ discharged by R319. Independent digital supplies on U3 prevent powered host pins from sustaining the unpowered reader-side output drivers. For the first build, operate on an open bench at 23 ±2 °C. Power each rail alone before enabling RF. [TI TLV755P](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv755p.pdf) · [TI ISO776x](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/iso7762.pdf)

* Five equal 10 kΩ feedback resistors plus a sixth 10 kΩ to ground make PORT_SUM equal to n/6 of PRE_3V3. With 1% resistors and one selection the interval is about 0.164–0.170 of the rail, between the 0.083 and 0.249 thresholds. Two selections give at least 0.329. The two window outputs and U305 can each sink RUN_VALID. R326 limits their combined request current to less than 1.6 mA. R321/R1 produce about 0.82 of RUN_REQ on a released ARM, over 2.4 V with a valid request. U2 requires at most 1.2 V high. A driven-low ARM also reaches host feedback U5. [TI TLV902x](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv9024.pdf) · [TI TPS22918](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22918.pdf)
* U305 supervises PRE_3V3 before releasing RUN_VALID. U303 supervises both the rail and RUN_VALID before releasing ARM. U306 independently requires the coil reservoir and RUN_VALID. None needs a nominal RC ramp to establish the minimum ARM delay. The three G33 devices trip near 3.07 V. Open CT gives a specified 12–28 ms release delay. U306's 0.405 V adjustable input, ±2% threshold, 25 nA sense error and 97.6 kΩ/10 kΩ 1% resistors give a falling trip of 4.19–4.53 V. Rising release includes hysteresis. A slow or weak supply can safely refuse to arm. Check COIL_RES and the actual pickup voltage before changing a power setting. [TI TPS3808 electrical characteristics](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3808.pdf)
* U203 compares the held ISO_GATE with COIL_RES/4 and drives U204 nSLEEP from the same supply. R209 biases sleep low. IN1 high / IN2 low pulls OUT2 down. K200 draws about 21 mA at 5 V. D204 provides a freewheel path. The nominal 5 V relay requires up to 4.0 V pickup at the specified coil temperature and up to 3 ms for operation, excluding bounce. Verify settled TX/RX contact closure in the minimum 12 ms ARM interval. For the latching relays, address 0–4 individually and apply 20 ms SET/RESET pulses only after TP301 indicates quiet. Both commands idle low. Invalid addresses enable no populated driver. [Omron G6K-RF](https://components.omron.com/us-en/products/relays/G6K-RF) · [TI DRV8837 logic and truth table](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv8837.pdf)
* When ARM is denied, R106 provides a 33.2 Ω ±1% discharge path independent of QOD. With a qualified upper bound of 20 µF on effective switched capacitance, 3.45 V falls below 0.30 V in 1.64 ms. At the upper rail R106 dissipates 0.362 W in a 1 W 2512. It adds about 100 mA during operation. Verify U1 input/output power and junction estimate. A 0.25 A regulator load at 4.9 V input dissipates 0.40 W. Reduce drive or duty if the temperature limits in the bench procedure are exceeded.
* D203 isolates C204 when J1 falls. With C204 at its −20% limit (376 µF), minimum U306 falling trip 4.19 V and total reservoir drain bounded at 30 mA, the stored rail takes at least 2.1 ms to lose 0.17 V. This leaves about 4.02 V for K200 and its conducting driver. On each unit check at least 4.00 V directly across the coil until the measured field is quiet. Require effective C205 ≥1 µF at 3.3 V DC and an initial ISO_GATE ≥2.1 V; even with two 100 kΩ discharge paths and 10 µA additional leakage the drop over 2.1 ms is below 0.12 V. The maximum comparator threshold is below 1.36 V. Validate the actual envelope, reservoir load and contact timing together. Coil mechanics, ceramic DC bias and residual RF are physical acceptance items.
* U304 holds COIL_OK low until the quiet comparator, envelope switch and qualified PRE_3V3 have all released it for at least 12 ms. RUN_REQ, RF_3V3 and ISO_GATE also charge QUIET_HOLD. The RF envelope FET can extend the inhibit. Its threshold varies, so low-level RF safety uses the discharge, isolated gate hold and measured contact timing. The supervisor's 0.4 V low is below the HCS decoder's low threshold on a qualified 3.3 V rail. A released 10 kΩ pullup drives its high input. The DRV8837 input guarantees low below 0.25 × VCC and high above 0.5 × VCC. During complete loss of PRE the decoder and port drivers lose their logic supply. Test every supply-removal combination into a dummy load, including PA present with the reader dead. [TI HCS238](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hcs238.pdf)

## RF operating envelope

| Population / patch | Limit and model |
| --- | --- |
| Direct, J201 → J202; J209 disconnected | Begin at the lowest PN5180 drive into a characterized 50 Ω load. R104 and R105 sit before the EMC filter. A bounded 3.45 V RMS differential source and at least 65.736 Ω series resistance can deliver at most 45.3 mW into any passive matching network, summed over harmonics. T201 and T202 each have a 250 mW maximum RF rating. Measure the actual IC differential voltage and overshoot. |
| PA commissioning, J201 → J210 and J211 → J202 | Independent regulated 24.0 V ±5%, 0.15 A current limit. J209 pin 1 positive, 2 ground, 3 unused. Set a 25 mW initial **maximum** forward-power target into 50 Ω. At J210 limit the measured carrier to 1.50 V RMS and 4.25 V peak-to-peak, including overshoot. Limit forward power at J211 to 85 mW. Use only the on-board direct source in normal operation. |
| Installed panel | Begin at VNA power, then at the minimum direct drive. Advance toward the 25 mW commissioning target only after tuning, receiving and checking component voltages. The same 85 mW forward ceiling applies to the full assembly. Installed loading and trimmer heating can require a lower limit. No delivered power or distance has been measured. |

The PA input presents about 49.8 Ω from two 24.9 Ω resistors and couples half of J210's voltage to +IN. Gain is 1 + 749/187 = 5.005. R214 49.9 Ω and a matched load halve the output again. At 1.50 V RMS input, nominal load power is 70.5 mW. Gain and termination tolerances leave operating margin to the 85 mW stop. An open circuit approaches twice the matched load voltage. A short dissipates about 0.30 W in R214 at that input. The 1 W part and the 1 W inactive-port terminations have headroom. A 50 Ω port at the limit has 2.06 V RMS incident and up to 5.83 V peak with full reflection. Require a directional measurement. DC supply current alone cannot establish RF power. [TI THS3491 gain, output swing and thermal data](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ths3491.pdf) · [Mini-Circuits ADT1-1+](https://www.mini-circuit.com/pdfs/ADT1-1.pdf)

U201 uses a single rail, capacitively coupled input/output and a bypassed midrail reference. At full 0.15 A supply limit the protected PA rail is at least 21.28 V with a 22.8 V source. R219 10 Ω and even the minimum 37.6 µF at C218 limit a 25.2 V step to 0.068 V/µs. Keep external wiring short and verify both polarities of the edge at U201 pin 7 against TI's 1 V/µs maximum. Ramp the bench supply and disconnect cold. Limit the PA rail discharge path to the designed input resistor when connecting instruments. A short directly at U201 bypasses this protection.

U202's LED receives over 6 mA at 3.05 V even using the datasheet's conservative 1.65 V forward drop and a 1%-high 220 Ω resistor. R216/R217 are 12 kΩ / 2.7 kΩ. At high supply, their isolated transistor load remains below 1 mA where the 0.4 V saturation limit is specified at 5 mA LED drive. Even allowing the loaded midrail to rise to 0.55 × PA_POS and 1% resistors, PD exceeds REF by 1.60 V at the low rail, above the guaranteed 1.5 V enable. With the LED off, 25 µA bias through R217 produces less than 0.07 V, below the 0.8 V disable limit. The DDA exposed pad connects to ground. Verify the board's thermal impedance at operating duty; thermal figures from the datasheet use its specified test board. [Vishay VO617A](https://www.vishay.com/docs/83430/vo617a.pdf) · [TI THS3491](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ths3491.pdf)

The pickup samples the **selected bus** in both patches. R201/R202, T202, R203/R204 and 1 nF coupling feed the reader. Antiparallel BAS316 pairs limit each pin relative to VMID. For an imposed 5.83 V peak upstream, each 4.7 kΩ arm limits current to roughly 1.3 mA or less. That bound alone does not establish the IC's allowed injection or usable load-modulation sensitivity. Start at minimum drive, measure RXP/RXN and VMID, check open-circuit reflection and confirm recovery after each transmitted frame. Change both receiver resistors together if necessary. [NXP PN5180 RX input guidance, figure 48 and pin table](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/data-sheet/PN5180A0XX_C3_C4.pdf)

## Panel, native footprints and evidence

For an assumed 0.64 µH loop with 1 Ω conductor loss and R401 2.2 Ω, the example match calls for about 278 pF series and 898 pF shunt. At 85 mW accepted, with a 1 m cable, velocity factor 0.66 and 0.2 dB/m loss, the model gives 0.163 A RMS loop current, 6.88 V RMS series, 2.07 V RMS shunt and 58 mW in R401 (rated 250 mW). The fixed RF capacitors are 100 V C0G. The JZ200HV 350 V DC rating does not characterize RF current or dissipation. Measure both capacitor-bank temperatures and voltages under mismatch before increasing power. C405 remains an accessible coarse option. [Model](../../software/panel_match.py) · [Voltronics JZ-HV land and rotor drawing](https://www.knowlescapacitors.com/getattachment/Products/Capacitors/Trimmer/Half-Turn/JZ-Series/Knowles-JZ-Series-V-70513-JZ_High-Voltage-%281%29.pdf?lang=en-US)

The native library retains the A1 alias so symbols, sheets and footprints resolve locally. The [pin audit](RevA2/pin-audit.txt) checks 308 numbered pins on 32 active, switching, clock and magnetic devices using a fresh XML netlist. Package references include NXP SOT618-1 for U101, TI DSG0008A for the drivers, DDA0008B for U201, DBV variants for regulators/supervisors, DW-16 for isolators and PW for the comparator/decoder. The Omron custom footprint uses the manufacturer's top view, pins 9/10 for the shield and the second contact pole for state feedback. The transformer uses case CD542 with winding dots at 3/4. The SMA contacts are through-hole with four mounting/ground posts. C204 is 8 mm diameter/3.5 mm pitch and C218 is 6.3 mm/2.5 mm. Pin 1 of both radial footprints is positive. [NXP package](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/package-information/SOT618-1.pdf) · [Omron RF land pattern](https://components.omron.com/sites/default/files/datasheet_pdf/K242-E1.pdf) · [Amphenol RF 132134-14](https://www.amphenolrf.com/132134-14.html) · [Panasonic FR series](https://mediap.industry.panasonic.eu/assets/imported/industrial.panasonic.com/cdbs/www-data/pdf/RDF0000/RDF0000C1266.pdf)

The [sourcing table](../../hardware/RevA2-sourcing.csv) associates each order code with the local land and manufacturer. The factory list uses exact JLCPCB MPN matches and corresponding LCEDA pad-number geometry. The rotation correction aligns the vendor's numbered top-view pads with the native land; the listed offset corrects its placement origin. For U201 the maximum pad-center difference is 0.437 mm along the length of a 1.775 mm gull-wing land. Pitch and numbering align. Unmatched part codes, unavailable CAD geometry and through-hole parts have explicit local assembly dispositions. Compare the portal's graphic to the numbered assembly drawing. [JLCPCB parts library](https://jlcpcb.com/parts) · [Example factory library record](https://lceda.cn/api/products/C19653/components?version=6.5.51)

Independent CAM inspection measured outlines and drill diameters in the ZIPs and matched every L401 centerline stroke and polarity. Visual plots show an unbroken loop around the clear aperture, the local rear SMA return, a continuous controller L2 reference and four unplated mounting holes on each board.
