## Controller limits and bench sequence

The first label check can run on the existing vendor-compatible PC/SC reader and a single label. To exercise the electronics before routing the controller, use a PN5180 evaluation board for SPI and direct-drive measurements. On a separate ground-plane fixture, test K200, its hold-up circuit, one selector channel and the downstream pickup into a 50 Ω termination. Drive the PA on its own evaluation fixture first. Recheck all results after integration because ground return, contact capacitance and routing affect them.

1. **Power and interlock.** Use a current-limited 5 V bench supply at J1, independent 3.3 V host I/O and an open J2. Check the raw rail, diode drop, PRE_3V3, RF_3V3 and reset. F1 is a 0.50 A hold PTC. Budget regulator and PCB heating using the measured input current. At 4.7 V into the LDO and 250 mA, its example dissipation is 0.35 W. NXP specifies 250 mA maximum recommended TVDD current under its stated thermal conditions. Measure the reader supply and regulator temperature separately. [NXP PN5180, tables 123–124](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/data-sheet/PN5180A0XX_C3_C4.pdf) · [TPS22918](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps22918.pdf)
2. **Select the path cold.** Keep RUN low and SET/RESET low. With RF decay verified and TP301 high, address ports 0–4 individually. Issue 20 ms RESET pulses, verify all five J5 bits low, then SET only the desired port for 20 ms and return both commands low. Use a settled second-pole readback and continuity on the actual RF pole. Unpowered latching relays retain their last position. Switching time is specified up to 3 ms and the minimum coil pulse is 10 ms. Nominal coil current is 21.1 mA at 5 V. Keep a per-port count. Omron specifies 100,000 electrical operations under its test conditions. Set a service interval from the actual switching workload and contact checks. [Omron G6K-RF, standard package](https://components.omron.com/us-en/sites/components.omron.com.us/files/datasheet_pdf/K242-E1.pdf)
3. **Verify shutdown and brownout.** Five equal sense resistors plus a grounded sixth give PORT_SUM = n × PRE_3V3 / 6 for n set relays. The hardware window spans approximately 0.083 to 0.249 of PRE_3V3. Only one contact is inside. Test zero, two, all, each legal single, host reset, open J2 and removal of each supply. The decoded address selects the coil to pulse. The host must compare the full five-bit position with its requested port before running.
4. **Scope the order.** A qualified RUN energizes normally open K200 before ARM rises. The 100 kΩ × 470 nF network gives a nominal 33 ms delay to a half-supply threshold. K200 breaks the TX patch and downstream RX sample at rest. Its 470 µF coil reservoir has a nominal 111 ms time constant into 237 Ω. Actual release also depends on Q200 gate hold, the diode and the coil dropout voltage. D315 includes the gate hold in the inhibit circuit. A passive RF detector drives an insulated gate at Q301. Its threshold depends on diode and transistor characteristics. Measure the lowest field it detects. The independent timing hold must protect the interval below that threshold. Probe TP301, TP302, K200, both coil leads, J202 and RF_3V3. Require K200 settled before reader enable, field decay before K200 opens or any selector movement, and all five selector coil currents zero while RF is active. Test contact bounce and fast off/on cycles. Check R205 pulse energy and capacitor inrush at the 5 V limit.
5. **Prove absence of backpower.** With host only, PA only and both present, and with RF disabled, check PN5180 rails, TX1/TX2 and RXP/RXN. Repeat at brownout, with a bounded 13.56 MHz signal at J201 and J202 and with the largest expected reflected signal. Record both voltages and current into the switched domain, scope noise floor and any residual carrier. As a bench acceptance target after discharge, require RF_3V3 below 0.1 V, no sustained carrier above the measured fixture noise floor, and terminal voltages and injection within the device limits throughout the transition. Stop at any violation. Contact isolation, host I/O isolation and the PA shutdown input each need independent tests.
6. **Direct commissioning.** Remove PA power, patch J201 to J202, and start with a characterized 50 Ω load. T201 uses the ADT1-1+ 1:1 transformer. Its 0.25 W RF rating is an absolute limit. Measure forward and reflected power and harmonics before attaching the panel. The 470 nH/68 pF EMC arms and 300 pF/160 pF matching network are starting values. Measure the differential load and TX current at fixed low drive, then retune. Verify the 27.12 MHz clock, reset, and six EEPROM version bytes with the Pi probe. [NXP antenna design](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/application-note/AN11740.pdf) · [Mini-Circuits transformer](https://www.mini-circuit.com/pdfs/ADT1-1.pdf)
7. **Receiver and modulation.** K200 takes the sample after the active patch. R201/R202 (330 Ω/4.7 kΩ) give an unloaded fraction of 0.934. T202 loading and PCB parasitics change that ratio. Each leg includes 4.7 kΩ, 1 nF AC coupling and a BAS316 clamp pair to VMID. Measure both PN5180 pin peaks, DC bias, VMID movement, sideband amplitude and successful replies at minimum and maximum drive. NXP describes a desired peak of 1.5–1.65 V and a 1.8 V maximum dynamic input. A weak direct signal may underdrive the receiver and clamp conduction can distort replies. Adjust pickup and series attenuation on the fixture before final layout. Measure Type A modulation depth, rise/fall and overshoot into the actual cable and panel. [PN5180 §11.8.9.2 and table 139](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/data-sheet/PN5180A0XX_C3_C4.pdf) · [BAS316 characteristics](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/BAS316.pdf)
8. **Amplifier on dummy load.** Power off to repatch J201→J210 and J211→J202. Use current-limited ±15 V on J209 (positive, ground, negative). R211–R213 give 1 + (649+100)/187 ≈ 5 V/V. The 49.9 Ω series output halves that voltage into 50 Ω. At 0.20 V RMS measured at J210, nominal load power is 5 mW. At 1 V RMS it would be 125 mW, with another 125 mW in R214. These are ideal resistive-load calculations. Start at or below the first level. Check PD at pin 8 and separately test each split rail missing. Measure oscillation, enable/disable transients, supply current, THS3491 temperature, modulation and RX recovery before connecting a label or increasing power. [TI THS3491, DDA pins and PD thresholds](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ths3491.pdf)
9. **Actual label.** Start with a PC/SC reference reader and one physically identified label. Save the full device info, C1, orientation and a photo. For the custom board, implement and validate Type A activation, ATS-derived timing, ISO-DEP send/receive chaining and WTX before routing an image job through SPI. Retain the same path and field for transfer and refresh. Require separately recorded identity, every transfer acknowledgement, explicit completed status and a matching camera image. On field loss report the uncertain stage and inspect the screen before replay. [Vendor evidence](../research/VENDOR-PAYLOAD.md) · [Protocol reference](https://github.com/JUN-KOBAYASHI/ezsign-nfc-tools/blob/main/extras/PROTOCOL.md)

## Panel calculations and installed measurements

With an ideal 50 Ω secondary load, the controller starting match transforms to approximately 20.27 + j0.09 Ω across TX1/TX2 at 13.56 MHz. The differential calculation places 160 pF across the transformed load, two 300 pF capacitors in series with it, 34 pF across that branch (two 68 pF shunts to ground) and 940 nH in series (two 470 nH arms). Relay and transformer parasitics, the pickup and the actual driver impedance require measurement.

The etched 2 mm-wide, single-turn loop has a 140 mm square centerline, 5 mm corner chamfers and a 12 mm feed gap. The starting series bank is 270 pF plus 4.5–20 pF. The shunt bank is 820 pF plus 4.5–20 pF. C402 and C405 can add coarse capacitance. R401 is a 2.2 Ω 1206 damping resistor. Use an insulated tool on the trimmers. The JZ200HV rating of 350 V DC is insufficient to establish allowable RF current or heating. The fixed C0G parts are specified at 100 V DC. [Voltronics JZ-HV drawing](https://www.knowlescapacitors.com/getattachment/Products/Capacitors/Trimmer/Half-Turn/JZ-Series/Knowles-JZ-Series-V-70513-JZ_High-Voltage-%281%29.pdf?lang=en-US)

```sh
python software/panel_match.py --loop-uh .64 --loop-ohm 1 --length-m 1 --vf .66 --loss-db-m .2
python software/panel_match.py --loop-uh .64 --loop-ohm 1 --length-m 1 --vf .66 --loss-db-m .2 --series-pf 282 --shunt-pf 832 --accepted-w .1
```

The assumed effective 0.64 µH and 1 Ω plus R401 give an ideal 277.54 pF series and 897.71 pF shunt. The ideal shunt value is outside the fine-trim range of the starting population. A nominal extra 68 pF at C402 brings it into range for this example. Choose the real coarse values after measuring the installed loop.

For 282/832 pF and 0.10 W accepted at the panel, the second command yields 0.177 A RMS in the loop, 7.36 V RMS across the series bank, 2.35 V RMS and 0.167 A RMS at the shunt bank, and 0.069 W in R401. Controller-plane |S11| is 0.071. Adding `--secondary .15 .4 3 34` changes |S11| to 0.192 in this example. This secondary is a hypothetical coupled mode with total impedance 3+j34 Ω. The single-frequency model needs measured effective L/R with ferrite fitted, installed cable loss and velocity factor, and the impedance reflected from each neighboring loop or wall mode. Measure mutual coupling between secondaries separately if it is appreciable.

Calibrate the VNA at the coax and record the reference plane. Start at instrument power. Save sweeps of the bare loop, cable, ferrite-backed panel and final mounting in the metal cavity. Repeat with all inactive ports terminated as fitted. Measure bandwidth across the modulation sidebands, inactive-port voltage, component heating and trimmer stress at the intended drive. Record cable type/length, wall clearance, ferrite material, product contents and orientation. Advance to the [coverage plan](PLAN.md) only after the same identity/transfer/refresh/camera sequence passes at close range. Fabrication outputs and final settings follow electrical and mechanical review.
