# Model inputs and evidence

All voltages, currents and H-fields in the calculations are RMS unless otherwise labeled. Frequency is 13.56 MHz. **Design** denotes a populated part or connection in the pinned A3 sources. **Published** denotes a manufacturer value with its conditions. **Assumed** denotes a scenario that the physical prototype or label could invalidate. Accessed November 5, 2026. The CSV sweeps are engineering examples without assigned probabilities.

The manufacturer's English manual, pp. 4 and 15 (SHA-256 `50edc5f784809e0e65d134570ffc38f132c4e174685d7ea083b6caca27e3cf35`), names SE0290C1 and specifies approximately 25 s full refresh. The online four-color product listing gives approximately 30 s per update. Neither source gives the power required during update. [Santek manual](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0276/2024/5581/files/EZ-Sign-User-Manual-Eng_compressed.pdf?v=1769634055)
