Components and operation

The PN5180 reader generates the RF signal and receives the display’s replies through the same antenna.

The XIAO microcontroller connects the reader to the vending computer over USB and manages NFC communication over SPI.

The tuning network matches the reader to the wire loop. Adjustable capacitors provide fine tuning for the installed antenna.

The loop powers the label by inductive coupling while image data is transferred and the display refreshes. The system then uses camera verification to check the visible result before customer pickup.
