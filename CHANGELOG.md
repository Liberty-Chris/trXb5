# Change log

## Rev A3 factory assembly, November 5, 2026

Both boards now have complete JLCPCB Standard assembly files. The controller includes the eight-pad latching relay, revised comparator routing, factory-inserted connectors and recentered placements. The panel uses a revised populated tuning bank. See the [A3 release](releases/RevA3/) and [engineering review](docs/validation/REV_A3.md).

## Rev A1 electrical review, November 5, 2026

Added native controller and 150 mm panel projects, review BOMs and schematic PDFs. Selected the PN5180 and captured vendor PC/SC command evidence. Added a Pi SPI probe, a conservative PC/SC label bench and an installed-panel calculation. See the [validation record](docs/validation/REV_A1.md) for check results, remaining controller routing and the physical tests required before fabrication.

## Initial baseline. November 4, 2026

Initialized the trXb5 development repository with requirements, architecture, evidence register, protocol research links, label observations, decision log, roadmap, KiCad handoff and blank bench templates.

Imported the original six sheet A0 target, editable SVGs and connection and BOM tables. Added a correction notice for the later Santek protocol evidence and the pending PN5180 versus ST25R3916B choice. No PCB, firmware or range claim is introduced by this initialization.
